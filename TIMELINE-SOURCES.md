# Timeline factual references

The timeline uses rounded flight durations, rather than exact station occupancy times.

- NASA, Skylab: A Chronology (SP-4011): program development, construction, crew dates and assignments, flight durations, and launch time.
  https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/sp-4011.pdf
- NASA, The Human Touch: The History of the Skylab Program: workshop conversion began in April 1969.
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20000012419/downloads/20000012419.pdf
- NASA, The Launch of Skylab, America's First Space Station: S-IVB-212 origin, launch preparations, and launch damage.
  https://www.nasa.gov/history/50-years-ago-the-launch-of-skylab-americas-first-space-station/
- NASA, Skylab 2 Astronauts Deploy Jammed Solar Array During Spacewalk: first-crew repairs and June 7 spacewalk.
  https://www.nasa.gov/history/50-years-ago-skylab-2-astronauts-deploy-jammed-solar-array-during-spacewalk-2/
- NASA, Joseph P. Kerwin: first American physician in space.
  https://www.nasa.gov/joseph-p-kerwin/
- NASA, Skylab 3: second crew and 59.5-day flight.
  https://www.nasa.gov/mission/skylab-3/
- NASA, Skylab 4: final crew, science, and planned departure.
  https://www.nasa.gov/mission/skylab-4/
- NASA, 45 Years Ago: Skylab Reenters Earth's Atmosphere: departure orbit, February 9 shutdown, proposed Shuttle reboost, and reentry.
  https://www.nasa.gov/history/45-years-ago-skylab-reenters-earths-atmosphere/
- NASA, Skylab Orbital Lifetime Prediction and Decay Analysis: July 11, 1979 reentry at 16:37 GMT.
  https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19810005468/downloads/19810005468.pdf
